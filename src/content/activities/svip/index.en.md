In 2017 I took part in a Silicon Valley internship program run by the Ministry of Science, ICT and Future Planning and the KAIST Moon Soul Graduate School of Future Strategy. After two months of entrepreneurship training and a selection process, I was chosen as one of 12 and did my internship at [**ZIBOT**](/en/works/zibot). The internship ran for a year through 2018, and I wrote up the experience at the time on a [**blog**](https://silinsta.wordpress.com/).
