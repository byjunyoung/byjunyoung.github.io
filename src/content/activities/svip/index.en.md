In 2017 I took part in the Silicon Valley Internship Program (SVIP), run by the Ministry of Science, ICT and Future Planning and the KAIST Moon Soul Graduate School of Future Strategy.

After two months of entrepreneurship training and selection, I was one of the 12 chosen and interned at [ZIBOT](/en/work/zibot), from February 2018 to January 2019.

I wrote about it at the time on a [blog](https://silinsta.wordpress.com/).
