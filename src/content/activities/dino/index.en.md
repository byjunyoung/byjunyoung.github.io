DINO is one of the best-known making clubs in the design department at [**UNIST**](https://www.unist.ac.kr/), planning and running a range of projects with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and the like. I joined DINO in 2021 while in graduate school at UNIST and took part in various making projects for about a year.
