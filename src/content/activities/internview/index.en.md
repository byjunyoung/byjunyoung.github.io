InternView was a short-run content project I did with the colleagues who came along on the [**Silicon Valley internship**](/en/activities/svip), to look at how hiring culture differs between the United States and Korea. To put Korea's cover letter culture under the eyes of people working in Silicon Valley in a more interesting way, we went to the CEOs and developers of the companies that shared our coworking space during the internship, introduced them to Korea's 'jasoseo' (personal statement) culture, and asked them to answer Korean 'jasoseo' (personal statement) questions, exploring job-seeking culture that way. On this project I led the whole effort, handling content planning and video production, and the results were uploaded to the [InternView YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/channel/UC97ypfun0Tm-McZTefrvyDQ?si=IxVxEQSbrXfjh5bK).
