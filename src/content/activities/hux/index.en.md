[**Seolgyejadeul**](https://uxdesigners.notion.site/2962d67da32c4f218fbea5944f612e60) is a community for practitioners working on hardware and auditory experience, started together with the co-organizers of [UXeed](/en/activities/uxeed).

I co-founded and ran the hardware experience group.

With about 20 practitioners and researchers, we held a regular book study and networking meetups.
