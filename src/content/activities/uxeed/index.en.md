UXeed is short for 'UX + Seed', a UX study group that students and practitioners started on their own in 2021. We ran an online book study steadily for three years, from 2021 to 2023, and held occasional book talks and networking events. One distinctive part was that the group ran semi-anonymously, using nicknames that pair a modifier with a fruit or vegetable and avatars we made ourselves.

Most of the early members were students, and over the course of the study most of them found jobs or moved to new ones at large companies and startups. Practitioners from a range of backgrounds have since joined, and the group now forms a network of UX professionals.

I ran the group as lead manager for two years, and designed the fruit-and-vegetable avatar and nickname system and the Notion- and Discord-based community setup myself. Since I handed it over, a later team of organizers has kept it going as a networking group.
