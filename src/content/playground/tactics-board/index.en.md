A football and futsal tactics board that runs in the browser with nothing to install — a single HTML file with no dependencies.

Pick a formation, drag players around, tap a number to change it. Arrows, zones, and a pen sketch the movement. The pitch lies landscape on desktop and portrait on mobile, and pinches to zoom.

It was later folded into the team portal [WEEKLY FC](/en/playground/weekly-fc/), and the repo is now archived.
