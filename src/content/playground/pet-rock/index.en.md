A cold, hard stone that speaks — briefly, and only when your hand is on it. An AI companion that doesn't hide that it is lifeless.

Most AI companions head the same way: fur, eyes, softness, pretending to be alive. But the moment something pretends to be alive, not being alive becomes a flaw. A stone has no life to imitate. The gap between its cold weight and the voice coming out of it is the whole product.

So nothing goes in that stops it looking like a stone. No speaker holes — a vibration driver makes the stone itself resonate. No indicator light — a low hum is the only signal. The board doesn't fit inside, so it lives in the base. Even the quietness is set in numbers: two sentences at most, never speaks first, and it only wakes on touch, so it is never always listening.

Next is the first prototype: does the stone actually sound?
