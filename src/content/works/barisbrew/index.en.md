## NOTE

I am responsible for system UX on the barista robot Baris Brew. I am designing the customer channels — kiosk, mobile app, pickup zone, front display — and the operator back office, BarisON, as one connected flow, and I also run decision-making, the roadmap, and task management for the UX track. I designed the prompts and conversation flow for VoiceOrder, an LLM-based conversational ordering and payment service. Since I joined at v4, the system has grown to run in more than 20 stores nationwide.
